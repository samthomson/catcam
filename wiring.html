Cat Cam wiring — everything on jumper cables

Three unconnected parts on jumpers — MB (USB power + flashing), ESP-32S-CAM (unstacked, pins free), HW-416 PIR — meeting at the breadboard: rails join +5V and GND, column 20 joins PIR OUT to GPIO13.

Shopping: 2× M–M (red, black) + 9× F–M (2 red, 2 black, 2 yellow, 2 blue, 1 purple) + 1 spare jumper for the boot strap. MB sockets are female → M–M; CAM/PIR pins are male → F–M. Wires 9–11 (flashing/serial) stay attached permanently.

Find every pin/socket by its printed label, not position (PIR pin order varies). CAM: use 5V, not 3V3; any of its several GND pins works.

2. The diagram

Your orientation: numbers 0→60 left-to-right, a–e / f–j top-to-bottom, gap and rails lengthwise. Only top rails + column 20 used.

Only filled dots ● connect — crossings arc over the board.

3. Assembly order

  1. PIR: both dials fully anticlockwise, jumper on H.
  2. Lay out MB, unstacked CAM, PIR around the breadboard. USB into MB.
  3. Wires 1–2: MB 5V/GND → rails.
  4. Wires 3–6: CAM + PIR power → rails.
  5. Wires 7–8: CAM 13 → d20, PIR OUT → e20.
  6. Wires 9–11: MB U0T/U0R → CAM U0T/U0R direct, CAM 0 → b20.

4. Flashing — in place, no disassembly

Wires 9–10 = permanent USB-serial link; wire 11 puts GPIO0 on the breadboard. GPIO0 LOW at power-up = bootloader.

  1. Spare jumper: a20 (or c20) → − rail — any free hole in column 20 works; each hole takes one pin, and all five are connected underneath (boot strap).
  2. Replug USB (boots into bootloader).
  3. pio run -e esp32cam --target upload
  4. Remove the spare jumper, replug USB. Wires 1–11 never move; column 20's other holes stay as they are.

Remove the strap to run — GPIO0 is the camera clock line (strap left on = black photo, no damage).

Monitor any time: pio device monitor. Once running, flash over WiFi instead (no cables, no strap): pio run -e esp32cam -t upload --upload-protocol espota --upload-port <device-ip>.

5. First run

After boot + WiFi + time sync: “Ready! Waiting for motion” — 30 s PIR warm-up, then wave:

--- Motion detected! Capturing photo ---
Upload successful! … Posted to Nostr!

≥5 s between photos; no new photo while one uploads; MB USB is the only power source.
